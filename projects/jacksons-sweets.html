THE PROJECT

A website that lets the products speak

Jackson's Sweets offers decorated desserts including cakesicles. The goal was to create a polished online destination where potential customers could learn about the business, explore previous orders, and find the information they need before reaching out.

Rather than crowding the experience with unnecessary elements, the site keeps the focus on product photography, clear navigation, and useful ordering details.

THE EXPERIENCE

Designed around real customer questions

Explore the work

A gallery of previous creations helps visitors see the range of desserts and decoration styles. Larger image viewing makes it easier to appreciate the details.

Understand the options

A separate pricing page explains cakesicle quantities, available specials, and decoration costs, helping customers plan their orders.

Know where to order

The website communicates that Jackson's Sweets serves Columbus, Georgia, including local delivery, so visitors have a clearer understanding of the service area.

Browse comfortably

Responsive layouts make the site easier to navigate on phones, tablets, and larger screens.

DESIGN APPROACH

Let the brand stay front and center

The website supports Jackson's Sweets as an existing dessert business. Its presentation is built around the business's own products, photography, and recognizable identity rather than an unrelated visual concept.

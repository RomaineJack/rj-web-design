THE PROJECT

More than a news website

Off The Sticks Press is an entertainment publication built around gaming, movies, and television. Its digital presence brings together news, reviews, guides, features, and community focused editorial content.

The website project goes beyond a standard brochure site, with a publishing workflow and custom media oriented interfaces developed to support content creation and management.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Distinctive from the first glance

Visual identity

The established OTSPress identity combines black backgrounds, white typography, electric lime accents, bold uppercase lettering, and high energy graphic treatments. This identity is preserved, not replaced.

Editorial design

Supporting social and editorial graphics explore consistent treatments for news, reviews, features, and entertainment coverage. New template concepts are presented as creative explorations rather than existing published campaigns.

DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

Built for a working publication

Public website

Editorial sections provide a place for readers to discover coverage across games and screen entertainment.

Publishing and media tools

The project includes custom administrative and media analysis workflows, such as content management and media review interfaces. Features may evolve as the platform is developed.
